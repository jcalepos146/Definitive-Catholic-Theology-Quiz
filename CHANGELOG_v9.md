# v9 Content Rationalization

## Question-bank changes

Ten historical question IDs are retired without renumbering the bank: **Q2, Q3, Q6, Q7, Q22, Q30, Q42, Q49, Q57, and Q97**. Their useful distinctions are absorbed into retained prompts, while the source entries remain for compatibility with old links and saved/challenge data.

Key mergers include Scripture hermeneutics + multiple senses, De Auxiliis + grace/freedom, motive of the Incarnation, justification + sanctification, sacramental causality + *ex opere operato*, Vatican II + hermeneutic of continuity, papal authority + historical development, and non-definitive magisterium + resistance to Roman directives. Q30, a direct theological self-identification prompt, is retired to reduce answer priming.

## New high-value questions

Eighteen questions were added: English Bible translation for liturgy; biblical inerrancy; the Vulgate; Scripture and apostolic Tradition; development of doctrine; nature and grace / natural desire for God; historical Adam and human origins; the Mass as sacrifice; baptism of desire; unbaptized infants; *subsistit in*; episcopal conferences; Latin and the vernacular; sacred music; the Roman Canon; conscience; ecclesial status of Orthodox and Protestant communities; and perfect contrition.

The existing English Bible translation question was rewritten specifically around **serious study**, so study and liturgical translation are now separate judgments.

## Anti-priming answer display

Answer propositions are now displayed without explicit school/tradition labels such as “Thomist,” “Molinist,” “Augustinian,” or “Reformed” when those labels function as identity cues. Personal theologian names such as Aquinas or Scotus may remain where they are substantively part of the formulation.

After a response, the per-option feedback no longer says which schools the option represents. It may instead show **representative theologians / historical witnesses** plus a generic doctrinal-status note. Scoring associations remain internal. The copy-prompt and answer-review text also use the de-primed proposition text.
